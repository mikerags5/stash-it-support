Privacy Policy

Stash It

Effective September 27, 2026

Stash It is a personal storage and organization app. Your privacy is simple to explain because we collect nothing and have no servers.

Your data stays yours

Everything you put into Stash It (items, places, photos, values, tags, and notes) is stored on your device and, when you're signed into iCloud, synced between your devices through your own private iCloud database (Apple's CloudKit). That sync happens entirely inside your personal iCloud account: we cannot read it, receive it, or access it in any way. If you're not signed into iCloud, everything simply stays on the device.

No collection, no tracking

  • We do not collect any personal information.
  • We do not use analytics, advertising, or tracking SDKs of any kind.
  • We have no servers; there is nothing for us to receive.

Camera and photos

Stash It asks for camera access only to photograph your items and scan the QR labels you print for your storage places. Photos you take or pick stay inside the app's storage (and your private iCloud sync, as above). If you export a QR label or save a photo, it goes only where you choose to share it.

When you use Rapid Add, Stash It can suggest names for a photo (for example "Camping Stove", or text it reads off a labeled box). That analysis runs entirely on your device using Apple's Vision framework; the photo is never uploaded anywhere for it.

Search, Spotlight, and Siri

So you can find things from the Home Screen, Stash It adds the names, locations, and small thumbnails of your items and places to your device's on-device Spotlight index, and offers a Siri / Shortcuts action ("where is my …"). Both are handled by iOS on your device; nothing is sent to us.

Backups and exports

Backup files (.stashbackup) and CSV exports are created on your device and shared only through the share sheet, to destinations you pick. Before a restore or CSV import replaces your data, Stash It saves an automatic safety backup in its own folder in the Files app (On My iPhone / iPad › Stash It › Backups) so you can undo it; you can delete those files at any time. We never see any of them.

Deleting your data

Deleting the app removes all of its data (including its Spotlight entries and safety backups) from that device. Your synced copy remains in your iCloud until you remove it (Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Stash It). There is no account with us and nothing retained anywhere else.
