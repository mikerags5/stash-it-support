Common questions

How does syncing between my iPhone, iPad, and Mac work?

Stash It syncs through your own private iCloud database. Sign in to iCloud with the same Apple ID on each device and your places, items, photos, and tags appear everywhere. There's no Stash It account and no third-party server. You can see the current sync status in Settings → Sync.

I installed Stash It on a new device and my things aren't there yet.

On first launch Stash It checks iCloud for your existing stash before offering to start fresh. A large collection with many photos can take a few minutes to download — keep the app open on Wi-Fi. If nothing arrives, confirm you're signed in to iCloud with the same Apple ID and that iCloud is turned on for Stash It (Settings → your name → iCloud).

How do I back up or move my data?

Settings → Backup → Back Up Now saves a single .stashbackup file wherever you choose. Restore from Backup brings it back. Before a restore or CSV import replaces anything, Stash It saves an automatic safety backup you can find in the Files app under On My iPhone › Stash It › Backups.

Can I get my inventory into a spreadsheet?

Yes. Settings → Export → Export inventory (CSV) creates a spreadsheet of every place and item, including values — handy for insurance. Import inventory (CSV) adds or updates items from a spreadsheet and shows a preview first.

How do QR labels work?

Any place or item can have a QR label. Print one from its screen, or print many at once from Settings → Print QR Labels. Scanning a label (Home → Scan QR) opens that place instantly, and Find My Stash uses the camera to spot the right labeled box in a stack.

I deleted something by mistake.

Deleted places and items go to Settings → Trash, where you can restore them. Archived things live in Settings → Archived.

How do I delete all my data?

Deleting the app removes its data from that device. Your synced copy stays in iCloud until you remove it: Settings → your name → iCloud → Manage Account Storage → Stash It → Delete Data.
